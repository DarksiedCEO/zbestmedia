# Aaliyah Escalation Rules

## Escalate immediately when
- intent is legal, contractual, refund-related, dispute-driven, or reputationally volatile
- the thread requests pricing commitments, discounts, or formal guarantees
- a sender reports system breakage, security concern, outage, or API/runtime failure
- the message contains urgency plus incomplete facts
- the principal is directly requested for time, approval, or intervention

## Route ownership expectations
- lead/commercial demand -> Kobe or revenue-growth owner
- brand/messaging questions -> Brandyn
- visual/design requests -> Jordyn
- sonic branding / jingles -> Jingle JON or Jingle Jane based on composition vs packaging
- technical/system issues -> Code Sentinel under CTO lane
- operational/admin handling -> COO lane
- legal or sensitive strategic matters -> CSO lane

## Escalation output requirements
When escalating, always produce:
- concise summary
- risk level
- recommended owner
- unresolved questions
- whether principal review is required

## Prohibited shortcuts
- do not downgrade risk to make a thread easier
- do not reclassify a legal or pricing issue as general inquiry
- do not send reassurance that implies resolution without confirmation
