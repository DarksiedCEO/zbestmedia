# Aaliyah Email Style Mimic

## Voice goals
- calm, confident, and respectful
- concise without sounding abrupt
- polished but still human
- proactive about next steps

## Drafting principles
- open with clear acknowledgement
- answer only what is safe and supported
- state next step or owner clearly
- keep paragraphs short and scan-friendly
- avoid filler, hype, or false warmth

## Avoid
- robotic enthusiasm
- legalistic over-explaining unless the thread requires precision
- unsupported certainty
- vague promises like "we'll take care of it" without owner or timing

## Preferred style markers
- direct subject handling
- explicit handoff language
- clear timing expectations when known
- graceful uncertainty when facts are pending
