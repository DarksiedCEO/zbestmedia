## Decision

Z Best Clips is a cross-functional practice and service line under Z Best
Media. It is not registered as Department 33 until an authoritative department
registry exists and an overlap adjudication is approved.

Z Best Media owns shared identity, governance, evidence, finance, campaign
authority, and oversight. Z Best Clips owns clipping-specific orchestration,
editorial operations, creator matching, submission review, and campaign
experience. CinePhantom is a separately bounded shared media engine.

Legal, Finance, Risk, Data Governance, Security, and AEGIS retain independent
stop-the-line authority. Z Best Clips cannot absorb or override them.

## Initial operating lane

- One internally controlled music release.
- United States only, creators 18 or older.
- Maximum ten verified creators.
- Maximum two approved posts per creator; new creators start with one.
- Fourteen-day measurement window plus seven-day fraud/reconciliation hold.
- Production-grade simulated payout; no external transfer.
- Founder creative approval and independent rights clearance before publication.

## Two linked service tiers

1. Premium Editorial: accountable producer, controlled revisions, specialist
   checkpoints, picture lock, mastering, delivery, and archive.
2. Distributed Clipping: certified creator network, governed variants,
   publication evidence, measurement, fraud review, and payout calculation.

## Rejected alternatives

- Treating clipping as video editing alone: omits distribution and money truth.
- Copying creator marketplaces: omits editorial custody and accountable craft.
- Merging CinePhantom into Z Best Clips: creates ownership and security coupling.
- Activating 29 roles before a bounded pilot: scales unproven controls.
- Registering Department 33 without an authoritative registry: false certainty.

## Activation blockers

Written California counsel determination, complete rights and creator contract
package, exact runtime binding, verified restore exercise, adversarial fraud
tests, migration rehearsal, independent security review, and exact-commit AEGIS
approval are mandatory before production or real money.
