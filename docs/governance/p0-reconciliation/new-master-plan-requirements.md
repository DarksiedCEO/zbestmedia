# New Master-Plan Requirements

These are locked architecture requirements, not active runtime claims.

## OOH/DOOH & Place-Based Media Division

Status: `NEW — REQUIRED — NOT BUILT`

- Initial market: Los Angeles DMA.
- Expansion path: Southern California → California → Western Region → National.
- Human executive: General Manager — OOH/DOOH, Western Region.
- Automation target: approximately 80%; expert human judgment: approximately 20%.
- Automated specialists: 23 total: 14 core planning/operations, 8 Proposal/Mockup/Margin Intelligence specialists, and 1 multilingual legal compliance/localization overlay.
- The 23 are additions to the future accountability ledger, not members of the current canonical 42-record Z Best Media census.
- Each must pass the complete source, contract, runtime, benchmark, Red Team, Sentinel, AEGIS, supervision, and live-mission chain.
- Legal advertising requires responsible-attorney approval; the system does not provide legal advice.

Required fail-closed domains include source provenance, inventory freshness, unit/client/language identity, measurement uncertainty, conceptual-rendering labels, margin floors, posting proof, billing duplication, and publishing authorization.

## Client Transaction, Contract & Confirmation Foundation

Status: `CROSS-AGENCY REQUIRED — NOT BUILT`

Required capabilities:

- contract lifecycle management;
- electronic signature evidence;
- document management and retention;
- billing and accounts-receivable state machine;
- signed, replay-protected payment webhooks;
- transactional email;
- automatic receipts;
- client portal records;
- transactional outbox;
- Embedded Sentinel monitoring and external escalation contract.

Required single-task roles: Contract Generation, Contract Integrity, Invoice Generation, Payment Reconciliation, Receipt & Confirmation, Document Retention, Client Notification, and Billing Exception.

No database field alone proves a signature or payment. Signed provider events, immutable hashes, idempotency, reconciliation, and delivery evidence are mandatory.
